# Brand identity

Full brand board: `brand/brand-guide.md` and `designs/brand/brand-board.png`.

## Tokens
| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| cream | `#F7F3EE` | default background |
| sand | `#EDE4D8` | case-study background, cards |
| ink | `#1F1D2B` | text; background for `update` posts |
| coral | `#E4572E` | accent: one word, number, or line per design |
| muted | `#8A8494` | footer, small labels |

Font: **IBM Plex Sans Arabic** (700 headlines, 400 body). Latin numbers and platform names stay Latin (CPM, ROAS).

## Layout rules (keep it calm)
- Max 3 elements per slide: label pill, headline, one short body. Lots of empty space.
- One coral accent per slide, never more.
- Pillar → background: `update` ink, `case` sand, everything else cream. This gives the grid a rhythm.
- Footer on every design: thin rule + three small icons (Instagram, LinkedIn, X) each followed by the **username only** (never a URL), muted color, ~22px. Handles come from `brand/handles.json`.
