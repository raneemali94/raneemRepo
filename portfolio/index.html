About me

Hi, I'm Raneem

I specialize in digital marketing and paid advertising. Over 4 years I have worked as a paid media manager and led a campaigns team. Most of my work has been with clients in Saudi Arabia and the Gulf, across e-commerce, real estate, education, services and hospitality.

What matters to me in every project is the real outcome: more sales and real customers, not just reach and engagement. That is why I always start with accurate tracking and tie every campaign to a clear goal the client can measure.

ROAS
Return on ad spend. A ROAS of 4 means every $1 in ads brought $4 in sales.
CPL
Cost per lead: what each potential customer who left their details cost.
CTR
Click rate: how many of every 100 people who saw the ad clicked it.
CAC
Cost to acquire a paying customer, not just a lead.
